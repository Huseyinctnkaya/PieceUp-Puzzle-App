# PieceUp Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 August 2026

PieceUp ("the app") is a Shopify app published by 34devs ("we", "us"). It shows
a jigsaw puzzle on a merchant's storefront and issues a discount code to
shoppers who complete it.

This policy describes what the app collects, why, how long it is kept and how
to have it erased. It is written against what the app actually does; if the app
changes, this page changes with it.

## Who is responsible for what

The merchant who installs PieceUp is the **data controller** for their
shoppers' data. We act as a **data processor** on their behalf: we only process
data to provide the app's functionality to that merchant, and we act on their
instructions.

If you are a shopper and want to exercise a right over your data, contact the
store you shopped with. They can pass the request to us through Shopify, and we
answer it within 30 days.

## What the app collects

### From shoppers

| Data | Why | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A random device identifier | To enforce the merchant's "one play per shopper" setting | Generated by the app, stored in the browser's `localStorage` |
| Shopify customer ID (only if signed in) | Same purpose, so the limit follows a shopper across devices | The storefront page |
| Which puzzle was played, when, and which prize was won | To show the merchant how their campaign is performing | The app |
| The discount code issued | To link a redemption back to the campaign that earned it | The app |
| Order ID, order total, currency, and the code used | To report to the merchant how much revenue the campaign generated | Shopify's `orders/create` webhook |

**The app does not collect shopper names, email addresses, phone numbers,
postal addresses, payment details or IP addresses.** Shopify's order webhook
contains some of these; the app reads only the fields listed above and discards
the rest without storing them.

The app does not use cookies. The device identifier is stored in
`localStorage`, is first-party, is never sent to any third party, and exists
solely to stop the same person claiming a reward more times than the merchant
allowed.

### From merchants

The store domain, the access token that lets the app call Shopify on the
store's behalf, the subscription plan, and the campaign settings the merchant
creates (puzzle images, prize names, discount values).

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or rent data to anyone.
- We do not share data with third parties for their own purposes. The app has
  no advertising, analytics or tracking integrations.
- We do not use the data to build profiles of shoppers or to make automated
  decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
- We do not send marketing messages to shoppers. The app has no mailing list.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Plays and attributed orders | 13 months, then deleted automatically |
| Campaign settings and daily counters | Until the merchant deletes them, or the app is uninstalled |
| Everything belonging to a store | Deleted when the app is uninstalled |

Thirteen months rather than twelve so that a merchant can compare a month
against the same month a year earlier. After that the records serve no purpose
and are removed by an automatic daily sweep.

When a merchant uninstalls PieceUp, we delete all of that store's data
immediately rather than waiting for Shopify's 48-hour redaction request.

## Your rights

Through the store you shopped with, you can ask to:

- **know what we hold about you** — we return every play linked to your
  customer ID: when you played, which prize you won and which code you were
  given;
- **have it erased** — we delete those records.

Erasure removes every link between you and any order. The order totals a
merchant uses for their own accounting remain, because they contain nothing
that identifies you: an order reference, an amount, a currency and a code.

Requests reach us through Shopify's `customers/data_request` and
`customers/redact` webhooks, which the app implements. Anonymous plays cannot
be included in either: a shopper who played without signing in is known only by
a random identifier that we cannot connect to a person.

## Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored encrypted at rest. Access to
production systems is limited to the people who operate the app. Discount codes
are generated with a cryptographically secure random number generator, are
valid for a single use by a single customer, and expire after seven days.

## International transfers

Data is processed on servers operated by our hosting provider. Where data is
transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the
European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, we update the date at the top and notify
merchants through the app.
